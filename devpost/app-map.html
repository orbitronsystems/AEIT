Global Agricultural Trade Intelligence, Events & Business Connectivity Platform

The Four Trade Arms Structure

ARM A — Manufacturers & Inputs

Machinery, precision irrigation, certified seed breeders, root-crop sorting, and greenhouse equipment.

HZPC (Netherlands) Caledonian Seed (Scotland) Netafim (Israel) GRIMME (Germany)

ARM B — Producers & Farmland

Commercial growers, outgrower farming co-ops, nurseries, and verified agricultural land leases.

Nile Delta Ag Co-op (Egypt) Al-Buhaira 450ha Lease Water & Soil Audits

ARM C — B2B Trade Desk

Cross-border traders, Incoterms, phytosanitary quarantine hazards, and advance cargo filings.

Agrico Cooperative Outeniqua Citrus (South Africa) CAPQ / NVWA Checklists

ARM D — Expos & Conferences

International agricultural machinery fairs and fresh produce summits with verified official links.

Agritechnica (Hannover) Fruit Logistica (Berlin) Sahara Expo (Cairo) NAMPO (South Africa)
